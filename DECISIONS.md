# Planned direction

- Build an intent-led legal document workspace: lawyers state their objective, then use a PydanticAI co-agent to investigate the document and bring cited findings into an editable, exportable report. This addresses the gap between getting answers from document chat and producing useful client work, while keeping legal judgment with the lawyer.
- Prioritise trust and continuity over more chat features: inspectable source passages, explicit uncertainty, user-approved amendments, and saved findings and reports. This makes AI work easier to verify and build on; organisation collaboration, version history, and explicitly saved preferences are future extensions outside the initial two-hour scope.
